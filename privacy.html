The short version

Everything you enter stays in this browser on this device. My Maps has no accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking. We never receive, see or store what you enter.

What is saved on your device

My Maps saves the following in your browser's local storage so it is still there next time:

  • The places you mark.
  • Your map appearance and color scheme.
  • Whether you have seen a holiday message.
  • When you closed the Add to Home Screen message.

Installing the app also keeps a copy of the site on your device so it works offline. Clearing your browser's site data or uninstalling the app removes everything, and we cannot recover it.

What your device tells My Maps

My Maps reads your device's time zone and language setting to format dates, numbers and units and to choose season and holiday colors. These settings are used only on your device and never leave it.

Files you export or share

Files you export, such as lists and images, are created on your device and saved where you choose. They are not uploaded anywhere. When you use Share, My Maps opens the app or website you pick with a link and a short message. Nothing is posted until you confirm it there, and that service's privacy policy then applies.

Where the site is served from

Every file the page uses, including its fonts and data, comes from this site. The page makes no requests to other services. GitHub Pages hosts the site, and Cloudflare provides its domain name service. Like any website, these services can see your IP address and basic browser details when you visit, under their own privacy policies. Neither receives what you enter.

Children

My Maps does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If My Maps ever starts collecting information, such as anonymous visit counts, this page will be updated first to explain what is collected and why. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent first.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to report[at]alltripsconsidered[dot]com.
