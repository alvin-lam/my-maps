Privacy

My Maps keeps your map on your own device.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no cookies and no tracking or analytics.
  • The places you mark and your settings are saved only in your browser, on your device.
  • We never receive, see or store your map.

What is saved on your device

My Maps uses your browser's local storage to remember the places you mark, your appearance settings and whether you have dismissed a message. Installing the app also keeps a copy of the site on your device so it works offline. This information stays on your device. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app removes it, and it cannot be recovered by us.

Where the site is served from

Every file the page uses, including the map tools, map data and fonts, comes from this site. The page makes no requests to other services. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its address is managed by Cloudflare. Like any website, these services can see your IP address and basic browser details when you visit, under their own privacy policies. Neither receives the places you mark.

When you share

The Share menu opens the network you choose, such as Facebook or WhatsApp, with a link to My Maps and a short message. The message may include how many places you have been to. Nothing is posted until you confirm it on that network, and what happens next is covered by that network's privacy policy.

Exports and downloads

Lists and images you export or download are created on your device and saved where you choose. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Children

My Maps does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If My Maps ever adds features that collect information, such as analytics, this page will be updated first and will explain what is collected and why. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent.
